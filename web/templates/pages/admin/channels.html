Telegram channels

Public channels are read from their web preview at t.me/s/name. New posts are fetched on a schedule. A meme that is already in the library, or looks the same, is not added again: the post link is added to that meme as another source. Videos longer than the configured limit are skipped. The whole history is loaded in batches of a few hundred posts; the number next to the button limits how many old posts to import (0 = all).
